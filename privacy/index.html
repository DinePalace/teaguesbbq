Privacy Policy

Teague's BBQ respects your privacy. This website is a simple, static informational site — we do not collect, store, or sell personal information through it.

Information we don't collect

We don't run accounts, logins, or contact forms on this site, and we don't set advertising or tracking cookies of our own.

Ordering & third parties

When you place an order, you're taken to our ordering partner ezCater, which handles your order and payment details under its own privacy policy. Anything you share there is governed by ezCater, not this site.

Contacting us

If you call us, we use your details only to respond and to serve your order or event — never for anything else.

Questions
