Frequently Asked Questions

Crawfish Boil Catering Details

How much crawfish should I order per person?

For average eaters with sides (corn, potatoes, sausage), we recommend 3–4 lbs per person. For dedicated crawfish lovers or seafood-only events, 5 lbs per person is ideal.

Can you customize the spice level?

Yes! We can tailor the heat from mild Texas flavor to authentic fire-breathing Louisiana spicy to suit your crowd.

What is required at the venue for an on-site boil?

All we need is an outdoor open space (patio, parking lot, backyard) and access to a standard water spigot for rinsing. We bring all our own propane, burners, and cooking gear.
