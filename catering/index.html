Plan the Right Amount

How Much BBQ Do I Need?

The question we get asked most. Here's the rule of thumb we use when planning spreads — and don't worry, we'll double-check your numbers when we build your quote.

What You're Serving Per Person Rule of Thumb Pitmaster's Note
Smoked Meats (one meat) About 1/3 lb per person Brisket lovers eat heavy — round up for hungry crowds
Smoked Meats (two or more) About 1/4 lb of each meat People like to sample everything on a multi-meat spread
Pork Ribs 3–4 bones per person More if ribs are the star of the show
Sides 4–6 oz of each side Two or three sides keeps every plate full and happy
Breads 1–2 pieces per person Nobody has ever complained about extra corn bread
Desserts 1 serving per person Trust us — people always find room for cobbler
Pitmaster's tip: Feeding a crowd of big eaters — football teams, work crews, or teenagers? Pad your headcount by 10–15%. Leftover Teague's brisket makes a great breakfast anyway.

Got Questions?

Straight Answers, No Runaround

The questions we hear most often. Don't see yours? Give us a call.

How far ahead should I book my catering?
The sooner the better — especially for weddings and the holiday season, when the pits book up quickly. That said, if your event is coming up fast, don't hesitate. Call us at (832) 606-3789 and we'll do our best to make it work.
How far will you travel?
We're based in Spring, TX and serve the whole surrounding area — The Woodlands, Klein, Tomball, Conroe, North Houston and beyond. Have an event a bit further out? Just ask — we've taken the smoker a long way before.
What's the difference between drop-off and full-service catering?
Drop-off means we deliver everything hot, set it up buffet-style, and then leave so you can enjoy your party. Full service means our crew stays to serve your guests, keep the line moving, and clean up afterward. Pickup and standard delivery are also available — whatever suits your event.
Can you handle allergies and special diets?
Absolutely. Tell us about any allergies or dietary needs in the special requests box on the quote form, and we'll plan the menu around them. We'll accommodate any reasonable request.
Is there a minimum headcount?
Every event is a little different, so we don't use one-size-fits-all rules. Tell us your headcount in the quote form and we'll build something that makes sense for your group — big or small.
How does pricing work?
No hidden fees, no surprises. You tell us what you want, and Matthew builds a custom quote around your menu selections, headcount, and service type. Final pricing is always confirmed personally — the old-fashioned way.
