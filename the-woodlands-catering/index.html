Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book catering for The Woodlands?

For standard drop-off deliveries and corporate orders, 24–48 hours notice is ideal (same-day orders may be accommodated via ezCater). For full-service weddings and whole pig roasts, we recommend reserving 1–3 weeks in advance.

Do you provide plates, cutlery, and serving utensils?

Yes! All catering orders include sturdy disposable plates, cutlery packs, napkins, tongs, and serving spoons so your event is 100% turnkey.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes, our smoked meats and several homestyle sides are naturally gluten-free. Contact our catering coordinator to discuss any custom allergen accommodations.
