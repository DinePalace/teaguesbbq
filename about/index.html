Our team

A one-stop shop for your food & catering needs

Teague's BBQ Catering & More is a one-stop shop for your food and catering needs. We have served Houston and the surrounding areas since 1989.

Our first catering event was for 300 guests, and we continue to provide quality food and services to our patrons — the same care, seasoning and low-and-slow patience on every plate.

Our meats are slowly smoked to perfection with the seductive scent of pecan wood and the taste of special seasonings throughout. We start with fresh meats and produce, using care in seasoning and preparation while investing the time to cook each item to perfection.
